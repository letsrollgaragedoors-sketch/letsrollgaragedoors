Garage door services in Austin

Let's Roll Garage Doors repairs and installs residential garage doors and openers across Austin and nearby cities, with a 24/7 emergency line. Estimates are free, and you approve the exact price before any work begins. Pick a service below, or see the cities we serve.

Repair

  • Garage Door Repair: Diagnosis and repair for doors that are noisy, stuck, crooked or not closing properly.
  • 24/7 Emergency Repair: On-call help when a door is stuck open, stuck shut or has dropped.
  • Same-Day Service: Same-day appointments for most repairs.
  • Spring Repair: Torsion and extension spring replacement. From $299 for one spring, from $499 for two.
  • Cable Repair: Frayed, slipped or snapped lift cables.
  • Roller Replacement: Replace worn or noisy rollers for a smoother, quieter door.
  • Off-Track Door Repair: Doors that have come off their tracks, safely reset or repaired.

Openers

  • Opener Repair and Installation: Opener repair, replacement and installation.
  • LiftMaster Openers: LiftMaster openers, our most common install.

Installation and maintenance

  • Garage Door Installation: New door installation and full replacement.
  • Garage Door Tune-Up: Inspection, lubrication, balance and safety checks.

Help guides

  • Garage Door Troubleshooting: Try these fixes first if your door is not working.
  • Manual Release Guide: How to open your garage door by hand.
  • Sensor Alignment Guide: How to align garage door safety sensors.

Brands

We install and trust LiftMaster openers most often, and we also service Chamberlain, Genie and Craftsman openers and doors from Clopay, Wayne Dalton, Amarr and other major manufacturers.
