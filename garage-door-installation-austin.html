04 · Repair or replace

Should You Replace Your Garage Door or Repair It?

Repair it if one part is broken and the door is in good shape. Replace it if the door is badly damaged, old and failing in more than one place, or costs more to keep fixing than it is worth.

Signs it is time for a new door:
  • Several panels are dented, cracked or sagging
  • The wood is rotting or the steel is rusting through
  • Repairs keep coming back
  • The garage gets very hot because the door has no insulation
  • You want a different look for the house

Not sure? Call us and we will tell you honestly. If a repair will do, we will say so. Our guide on when to replace a garage door goes into more detail, or see our garage door repair page.

12 · Questions

Garage Door Installation Questions, Answered

How much does a new garage door cost installed in Austin?

Most installed garage doors in Austin cost between $900 and $2,500 or more. The price depends on size, style, insulation and windows. We give you one price in writing before we start.

How long does garage door installation take?

Most single doors take about 3 to 5 hours, including removing the old door, installing the new one and balancing it. Double doors, and jobs with a new opener, can take longer.

What is the difference between garage door installation and replacement?

In most homes it is the same job. Replacement means we take out your old door and put in a new one. Installation also covers new construction, where there is no door yet. Either way we handle the whole job.

Should I repair or replace my garage door?

Repair it if one part is broken and the door is in good shape. Replace it if the panels are badly damaged, the door is old and failing in more than one place, or repairs keep coming back. We will tell you honestly which one makes sense.

Will a new garage door work with my existing opener?

Often yes. Heavier insulated doors sometimes need a stronger opener, and we check before we quote. If you need a new opener, we install LiftMaster openers.

Is an insulated garage door worth it in Austin?

For most Austin homes, yes. It matters most if the garage is attached, faces south or west, or sits under a bedroom. An insulated steel door with a polyurethane foam core keeps the garage noticeably cooler in summer, and it is stiffer and quieter than a thin door.

How soon can you install a new door?

We can often give you a quote the same day. Standard doors can usually be installed within a day or two of approving the quote. Special colors, sizes and custom designs can take longer because the door has to be ordered.

Do you remove and haul away my old door?

Yes. Removing and disposing of the old door, track and hardware is part of the job.

What warranty comes with a new garage door?

New doors carry the manufacturer's warranty, and our installation work is covered by our workmanship guarantee. Terms vary by door, and we go over them with you before we start.

Can I design my door before I buy?

Yes. Free design tools from DoorLink and CHI let you try styles, colors and windows on a sample home or a photo of your house. The links are in the Design Your New Door section above.

Which areas do you serve for garage door installation?

We install across Austin and nearby cities, including South, North, East and West Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Buda, Kyle, Manor, Leander, Georgetown, Lakeway, Hutto and Bastrop.
