An off-track garage door is a door where one or more rollers have moved out of the tracks that guide the door. The door may become crooked, stuck halfway, difficult to move, or completely unusable. At Let's Roll Garage Doors, we provide professional off-track garage door repair in Austin, TX, fixing both the immediate problem and whatever caused it.

We do not recommend forcing an off-track garage door back into position. Garage doors are heavy, and springs and cables can be under significant tension. Incorrect repairs can cause additional damage or injury. If your door is crooked, stuck, or visibly off track, stop operating it and have the system inspected.

Common Causes of an Off-Track Garage Door

Broken or Loose Cables

When a lift cable snaps or slips off the drum, one side of the door drops and the rollers can pop out of the track.

Damaged or Worn Rollers

Cracked, worn, or seized rollers bind in the track and can jump out under load, especially on older doors.

Bent or Damaged Tracks

A bent track section, from impact or loose brackets, gives the rollers a path out of alignment.

Broken Springs

A failed spring shifts the door's full weight onto the cables and tracks, which can pull the door out of alignment.

Impact or Accidental Damage

A bump from a vehicle bumper or equipment is one of the most common reasons doors leave their tracks.

Obstructions & Worn Hardware

Debris in the track or loose, aging hardware can gradually work a door out of alignment over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if my garage door comes off track?

Stop operating the door and avoid forcing it open or closed. If it is crooked, stuck, or visibly off track, contact a professional garage door technician.

Why did my garage door come off track?

Common causes include broken or loose cables, damaged rollers, bent tracks, broken springs, worn hardware, impact, or an obstruction.

Can an off-track garage door be repaired?

In many cases, yes. The repair depends on what caused the door to come off track and whether other components were damaged.

How long does an off-track garage door repair take?

Repair time varies depending on the cause and parts involved. Some repairs are straightforward, while damaged tracks or multiple failed components can take longer.

Is an off-track garage door dangerous?

It can be. Garage doors are heavy, and springs and cables may be under significant tension. Avoid trying to force or manually reposition a damaged door.

Does an off-track door need to be replaced?

Not necessarily. If the door is structurally sound, repairing the rollers, cables, tracks, or other hardware may be sufficient.
