Your garage door is the largest moving object in your home and most doors cycle over a thousand times a year. Springs stretch, hardware loosens, rollers dry out, and the opener quietly compensates until something finally gives, usually at the worst possible time.

Let's Roll Garage Doors offers garage door tune-ups and preventive maintenance in Austin, TX, a full-system service that keeps your door quiet, balanced, and safe, and extends the life of your springs and opener.

What's Included

Full Lubrication

Springs, rollers, hinges, and bearings lubricated with garage-rated silicone lubricant, the fix for most squeaks and grinding.

Spring Balance Test

We disengage the opener and test the door's balance by hand. An unbalanced door is the #1 cause of premature spring and opener failure.

Hardware Tightening

Every bolt, hinge, and bracket checked and tightened. Vibration loosens hardware a little more with every cycle.

Track & Roller Inspection

Tracks checked for alignment and dents; worn or wobbling rollers flagged before they jump the track.

Safety System Test

Photo-eye sensors and the auto-reverse mechanism tested to make sure the door stops and reverses like it should.

Opener Check

Force and travel-limit settings verified, chain or belt tension checked, and remotes and wall controls tested.

Signs Your Door Is Overdue

Squealing or Grinding

Dry rollers and bearings get loud before they fail.

Jerky or Shaky Travel

Uneven movement points to balance, track, or roller issues.

Heavy to Lift by Hand

A balanced door should lift easily with one hand, if not, the springs are losing tension.

Slower Than It Used to Be

An opener straining against a poorly maintained door slows down before it burns out.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a garage door be tuned up?

Once a year for most Austin homes. If your door cycles more than 5-6 times a day, or it's your home's main entrance, twice a year is worth it. Central Texas heat is hard on lubricant and weather seals, so many customers schedule before summer.

How long does a garage door tune-up take?

About 45 minutes to an hour for a standard residential door. If we find a worn part during the inspection, we'll show it to you and quote the fix before doing anything beyond the tune-up.

What's included in a garage door tune-up?

Lubrication of springs, rollers, hinges, and bearings; spring balance testing; tightening of all hardware; track alignment check; safety sensor and auto-reverse testing; opener force and travel-limit check; and a weather seal inspection.

Is a tune-up really worth it?

Yes, springs on an unbalanced door wear out significantly faster, and a strained opener works harder every cycle. A yearly tune-up is a fraction of the cost of an emergency spring replacement or a new opener, and it's the best way to catch small problems before they strand your car in the garage.

Can I just lubricate the door myself?

You can and should apply a silicone- or lithium-based garage door lubricant to rollers and hinges between visits (avoid WD-40, it's a solvent, not a lubricant). What a tune-up adds is the part you can't safely do yourself: testing and adjusting spring balance, which involves components under high tension.
