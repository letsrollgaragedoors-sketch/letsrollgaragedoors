If your garage door has gotten noticeably louder over time, worn rollers are usually the culprit. Standard steel rollers wear down with use and can cause grinding, rattling, or jerky movement as the door travels along the track.

Let's Roll Garage Doors offers garage door roller replacement in Austin, TX, including an upgrade option to nylon rollers, which run significantly quieter and require less maintenance than steel rollers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to replace garage door rollers in Austin?

A full set of standard steel rollers typically costs $100-$150 installed. Upgrading to nylon rollers usually runs $150-$250 depending on door size.

Are nylon rollers worth the upgrade?

Yes, for most homeowners. Nylon rollers run significantly quieter and last longer than steel rollers, especially valuable if your garage is attached to living space.

How often should garage door rollers be replaced?

Standard steel rollers typically need replacement every 7-10 years with normal use, though heavy daily use can shorten that timeline.

Can worn rollers damage other parts of my garage door?

Yes, worn rollers can cause uneven strain on tracks, hinges, and even the opener, so it's best to address them before they get worse.

Why is my garage door making a grinding noise when opening?

Grinding during travel is the classic sound of worn rollers - the ball bearings wear out, the roller stops spinning, and it drags through the track instead. Left alone, dragging rollers wear the track, strain the opener, and can eventually cause the door to jump the track. Roller replacement is a quick, affordable fix, and upgrading to sealed nylon rollers makes the door dramatically quieter.
