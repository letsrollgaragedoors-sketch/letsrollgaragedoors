Questions

Same-Day Service FAQs

Answers to what Austin homeowners ask us most about same-day repairs.

Can you really fix my garage door the same day I call?

In most cases, yes. Our trucks are stocked with common parts like springs, cables, rollers, and hinges, so most repairs are completed during the first visit, the same day you call.

What's the difference between same-day service and 24/7 emergency service?

Same-day service means we can get to most non-emergency repairs within the same business day. Our 24/7 emergency line is for urgent situations, like a door stuck open overnight, where you need help right away regardless of the hour.

How much does same-day garage door repair cost?

Cost depends on the issue. Spring repairs typically run $150-$350, opener repairs $100-$250, and other repairs vary based on parts needed. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

Do you charge extra for same-day appointments?

No. Same-day service is part of our standard offering at no additional rush fee for regular business hours. After-hours emergency calls may include a separate emergency service rate.

What areas do you offer same-day service in?

We offer same-day service throughout Austin and surrounding communities, including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Kyle, Buda, Lakeway, Leander, Hutto, Manor, and Bastrop.

What if my garage door problem turns out to be bigger than expected?

Our technician will inspect the door, explain what's going on, and give you a clear estimate before doing any work. If a full replacement makes more sense than a repair, we'll walk you through that option too.
