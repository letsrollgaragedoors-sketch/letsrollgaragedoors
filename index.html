How quickly can you respond to a repair call?
For standard repairs we offer same-day service. For emergencies, we aim to have a technician on-site within 60 minutes, 24/7, including weekends and holidays.
Do you offer a warranty on your work?
Yes. All repairs and installations include a workmanship guarantee plus manufacturer warranty on parts. We stand behind our work, if anything goes wrong, we'll make it right. See our full warranty terms for coverage details and how to file a claim.
Can you repair any brand of garage door or opener?
Absolutely. We service all major brands including Clopay, Wayne Dalton, Amarr, LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, and Craftsman. If it's a garage door, we can fix it.
Is it safe to use my door if a spring is broken?
No, a broken spring can damage your opener, the door, or cause injury. Stop using the door immediately and call us. We offer emergency spring replacement and can often come out same-day.
How long do garage door springs last?
Most garage door springs last 7-10 years or about 10,000 open/close cycles, whichever comes first. Austin's heat and humidity can shorten that lifespan, so we recommend a yearly inspection to catch wear before a spring breaks.
Do you offer same-day garage door repair near me?
Yes, we offer same-day garage door repair throughout Austin and the surrounding area, including Round Rock, Cedar Park, and Pflugerville. Call (512) 947-4451 and we'll get a technician to you as fast as possible.
