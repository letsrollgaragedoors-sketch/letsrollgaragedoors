Why It Matters

Why Annual Maintenance Pays Off

Think of your garage door like your vehicle, you wouldn't expect it to run for years without an oil change. Regular maintenance works the same way.

Improve Safety

Garage doors weigh 150 to 600+ pounds. We inspect cables, springs, hinges, tracks, and safety sensors to help protect your family, pets, and vehicles.

Prevent Expensive Repairs

Small issues like worn rollers or loose hinges become major repairs if ignored. Catching them early saves hundreds, sometimes thousands, of dollars.

Extend the Door's Life

Lubrication, balance adjustments, and hardware tightening help your garage door and opener last years longer than a system that's never serviced.

Questions

Tune-Up FAQs

Answers to what Austin homeowners ask us most about maintenance service.

How much does a professional garage door tune-up cost?

Pricing varies by door size and condition. Call for a free, upfront quote, our team is transparent about cost before any work begins.

How long does a garage door tune-up take?

A standard tune-up typically takes under an hour, covering a full inspection, lubrication, balance test, and safety checks.

What's included in a standard tune-up service?

A full safety inspection, balance test, spring and cable check, roller and hinge inspection, hardware tightening, lubrication, track adjustment, opener performance check, and auto-reverse safety test.

How often should I get my garage door tuned up?

Once a year is the standard recommendation for most households, similar to routine car maintenance.

Can a tune-up prevent an emergency breakdown?

In many cases, yes. Most emergency repairs are caused by components that were wearing out for months, weak springs, fraying cables, or worn rollers. A routine tune-up catches these before they fail.

Is a tune-up necessary if my garage door seems to be working fine?

Yes. Many issues, like a weakening spring or a misaligned safety sensor, aren't obvious during normal daily use. A tune-up catches these before they turn into a bigger problem.
