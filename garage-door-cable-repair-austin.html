Garage door cables work alongside the springs to lift and lower your door safely. When a cable snaps, frays, or comes off its drum, the door can become unbalanced, get stuck, or in severe cases, fall unexpectedly. This is a safety issue that shouldn't wait. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to replace a garage door cable in Austin?

Cable replacement typically costs $100-$200, depending on whether one or both cables need replacement and whether other hardware is affected.

Is a broken garage door cable dangerous?

Yes, a broken cable can cause the door to fall suddenly or hang unevenly, putting strain on the opener and creating a safety hazard. Avoid operating the door until it's repaired.

Can I replace a garage door cable myself?

We don't recommend it. Cables work under spring tension, and improper handling can cause injury. It's best handled by a trained technician.

How long do garage door cables last?

Most cables last 7-10 years with normal use, though they can wear faster in doors used very frequently or exposed to heavy moisture.

What should I do if my garage door cable came off the drum?

Stop using the door immediately, including the remote. A cable that has jumped or unwound from the drum usually means the door was obstructed mid-travel or a spring problem released the tension - and the door can now sit crooked, jam, or fall. Don't try to rewind the cable yourself; the spring system it connects to is under high tension. Unplug the opener, keep the area clear, and call (512) 947-4451. Re-seating or replacing cables is a same-day repair in most cases.
