Why do so many Leander homes need spring replacements?

Leander's rapid growth over the last decade means many homes were built with builder-grade springs rated for around 10,000 cycles. With daily use, those wear out in 5-7 years. The Hill Country terrain also means some homes have steeper driveways and heavier doors, which puts extra load on springs and accelerates wear. We recommend high-cycle springs (25,000+ cycles) for the best long-term value.
