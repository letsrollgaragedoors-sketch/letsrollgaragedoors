Privacy Policy

How Let's Roll Garage Doors collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Who we are

Let's Roll Garage Doors ("we", "us") is a garage door repair and installation company at 720 Airport Blvd #1280, Austin, TX 78702. This policy covers letsrollgaragedoors.com. Questions about it can go to letsrollgaragedoors@gmail.com or (512) 947-4451.

Information we collect

  • What you send us. When you submit the quote form we receive your first and last name, phone number, ZIP code, the service you need and any message you write. If you call, text or email us, we receive whatever you share, including your phone number or email address.
  • Referral code. If you arrive through a link that includes a referral code, we store that code in your browser so we can attach it to your request.
  • Automatically collected data. Like most websites, our analytics and advertising tools collect technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view and how you arrived at the site, usually through cookies or similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, schedule service, give estimates and follow up on work we have done.
  • To understand how the site is used and to measure and improve our advertising.
  • To keep the site secure and to meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business and the website, and only as needed:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers quote-form submissions to our email.
  • Google, for site analytics and advertising measurement (Google tag) and for fonts loaded from Google Fonts.
  • Microsoft, for advertising measurement and site analytics (Microsoft Advertising UET and Clarity).
  • Our hosting provider, which serves the website.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it or to protect our rights, customers or property.

Cookies and tracking

The analytics and advertising tools above use cookies and similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com. Blocking cookies will not stop you from using the site or calling us.

How long we keep it

We keep customer contact and job information for as long as we need it to provide service, honor our warranty and meet business and legal requirements, and then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you by emailing or calling us at the details above. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to customer information to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
