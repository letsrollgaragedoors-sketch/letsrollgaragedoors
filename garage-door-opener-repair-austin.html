Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't my garage door opener respond to the remote?

This is often caused by a dead remote battery, a misprogrammed remote, or interference from nearby radio devices. If a new battery doesn't fix it, the logic board or receiver may need repair.

Why does my garage door opener hum but the door doesn't move?

This usually means the drive gear has stripped or the belt/chain has slipped or broken. The motor is running, but the mechanism connecting it to the door isn't engaging.

How much does a new garage door opener cost installed?

Installed pricing starts at $459. Chain drives are most affordable, while smart Wi-Fi belt-drive openers run higher.

Can you reprogram my garage door remote without replacing the opener?

Yes, in most cases reprogramming takes just a few minutes and is included in any service call.

Why is my garage door opener not working when the door works fine manually?

If the door lifts smoothly by hand but the opener won't move it, the problem is in the opener system, not the door. Check in this order: (1) the trolley - if someone pulled the red release cord, the opener runs but nothing moves; re-engage it by running the opener until the trolley clicks back in; (2) power - confirm the outlet works; (3) the safety sensors - a blinking sensor light means misalignment. If those check out, it's likely a worn gear, circuit board, or motor issue. We'll tell you honestly whether repair or replacement makes more sense.

What does it mean when the garage door opener light blinks?

A blinking opener light is a diagnostic code. The most common meaning: the safety sensors are blocked or misaligned - the door won't close and the light flashes when you try. Clear anything in the door's path and check that both sensor units near the floor show solid indicator lights. If the blinking continues, count the flashes and check your opener's manual (each brand uses different codes), or call us and we'll decode it over the phone.
