About Us

About Let's Roll Garage Doors

Locally owned garage door repair and installation in Austin, TX.

Meet Dudu Elmakies

Dudu Elmakies is the owner and lead technician of Let's Roll Garage Doors. He personally handles garage door repairs and installations across the Austin area, and the guides on this site are published under his name, so the advice you read here comes from the same person who may show up at your door.

Dudu has six years of experience in garage door repair and installation, and he has lived in Austin for 12 years, so he knows the area's homes, neighborhoods and Texas heat first-hand.

How we work

  • Diagnose first. We find the actual cause before recommending anything. A broken spring, a bent track or a worn roller can make a perfectly good opener look like it is failing.
  • Upfront pricing. You approve the exact price before work begins, and estimates are free with no obligation.
  • Test before we leave. Every job ends with a check of the door's balance, safety reversal and smooth operation.
  • Insured and background-checked. Let's Roll Garage Doors is insured, and every technician is background-checked.
  • Straight answers. If a repair is enough, we say so. If replacement makes more sense, we explain why.

What we do

We repair and install residential garage doors and openers, with same-day service for most repairs and a 24/7 emergency line.

  • Garage door repair, including off-track doors and doors that won't open
  • Spring replacement and cable repair
  • Opener repair and installation
  • New door installation
  • Tune-ups and maintenance
  • 24/7 emergency service

Brands

We install and trust LiftMaster openers most often, and we also service Chamberlain, Genie and Craftsman openers and doors from Clopay, Wayne Dalton, Amarr and other major manufacturers. Read our opener guide for how we help homeowners choose.

Where we work

We serve Austin (South, North, East and West) and nearby cities including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Leander, Lakeway, Kyle, Buda, Hutto, Manor and Bastrop. Our technicians are dispatched from our Austin location.
