When a Garage Door Bends at the Top

On a recent Round Rock call, the customer said the door was getting stuck and bending in on the way up. We found a bent top panel on a 9-by-7 door. We replaced the panel, reinforced it with a strut, installed new nylon rollers and did a door tune-up.

On most doors the opener pulls from the top panel, so that panel takes the most stress. If it flexes, or has no strut, it can bend, and a bent panel can catch in the track and make the door stick. A strut is a metal brace that keeps the panel straight. Read about our roller replacement and tune-up service.
